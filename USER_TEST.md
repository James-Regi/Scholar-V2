## Three-minute test protocol

1. Give the tester only the public URL.
2. Ask: “Please enter the game and play without me explaining the controls.”
3. Observe whether the tester can start, move, attack, understand enemy health, collect a drop, and use a special.
4. Ask: “What was confusing or frustrating?”
5. Record the observed friction in the tester's own meaning, then make and verify one focused revision.

## Existing creator-observed friction (not a substitute for the unfamiliar-user test)

During development, creator testing identified four concrete problems: the Enter button initially failed, Space attacks crashed the loop, enemy health bars drew left after passing zero, and Round 3 enemies arrived too quickly. Each issue produced a published revision and browser verification. These observations demonstrate iteration, but the completed submission note above should come from an unfamiliar user.
